This this the changelog file for the Pothos Audio toolkit.

Release 0.4.0 (2026-10-31)
==========================

- Needs the Pothos core 2.0.0 (the pothos2 line, ABI 2.0-0)
- Port to the modernized Pothos core (branch pothos2): no Poco
- miniaudio (a copy of miniaudio.h 0.11.25 in third_party/miniaudio) in
  place of PortAudio; the build needs no audio packages
- The device converts the sample format, the channel count and the
  sample rate; rxRate gives the rate of the stream
- Device names come from miniaudio: a saved PortAudio name gives an
  error; the device lists of the source and the sink are separate
- /devices/audio/info: the keys "Audio Device", "Backend Name" and
  "miniaudio Version"
- POTHOS_AUDIO_BACKEND selects the miniaudio backend (null for tests)
- The sample rate must be a positive number (0, a negative rate or NaN
  is an error); a rate that is not a whole number is rounded
- /devices/audio/info: "Native Sample Rates" (the rates of the native
  formats) in place of "Default Sample Rate"
- deactivate() without setupStream() does nothing; work() produces and
  consumes the frames that were moved; one atomic word holds the
  overflow and underflow count; on Windows the 1 ms waits of work() use
  a 1 ms timer period
- The doc text of the source says "default input device", of the sink
  "default output device"
- The self-tests unset the POTHOS_* variables of a developer shell
- The sink starts to play with a margin of 2 periods in its buffer (or
  after 2 periods of a short stream), and reports no underflow before
  that; one report for each overflow or underflow; the backoff time
  starts at the report
- CMake 3.25 or higher; nlohmann_json from find_package
- Add self-tests on the null backend of miniaudio
- The debian packaging is removed
